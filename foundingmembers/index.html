A letter to our founding families · November 2026

Thank you for building this with us.

Hello, founding families.

Thank you for joining us, and for choosing to build this club together with us, before there was anything to see.

The more we listened to you, and the more we sat with our architect and design team, the clearer one thing became. Since we are starting again from the ground up, we need more than a list of rooms. We need a philosophy: an idea strong enough to shape every space and every way we spend time together.

Ours rests on four pillars: calm, curiosity, creativity and courage. On this page you will see how your ideas, from the family kitchen to the tree houses, the co-working space to the film studio, have found their place under them.

With warmth, Ida, and the team at The Bury

01
Calm

Rest is where courage begins.

Nobody dares when they are exhausted. So before anything else, The Bury has to be a place where parents can breathe: work in peace, eat a meal while it’s still warm, and come back to their children ready to play.

Concept film

Co-working: work properly, stay close.

A quiet, calm place to get real work done, with call pockets and meeting rooms. It opens off the library, so when you close the laptop you are a few steps from your children, ready to reconnect.

We are still working out how quiet each corner should be, and how close is close enough. Tell us what a good working day here looks like for you.

The café

Coffee, lunch and something sweet from Nena Baker. Order to your desk while you work, or grab a seat by the window.

A relaxing meal, at last

Children head to the pay-as-you-go crèche or the central activity area, and you enjoy dinner. Pop over to feed them when they need you, then come back to your own plate.

02
Curiosity

Why a library sits at the heart of the house.

We chose the library as the connective tissue of The Bury: the room you walk into first, and the thread that links every other space. It won’t be one quiet room. It will have quiet corners, chatty corners and playful corners, so very different moods can live side by side.

  • Quiet corners. Silent reading nooks for children, and call pockets for parents.
  • Chatty corners. Long tables for talking, sharing a book, or meeting another family.
  • Playful corners. Low shelves, floor cushions and places where noise is welcome.
  • Two heights everywhere. What a child can reach, a child may touch.
  • Sightlines, not walls. A child can always find a parent’s face.

None of this is fixed. We are still finding the connections with our architect, and we would love your thoughts.

03
Creativity

Make a mess, make something real.

Creativity is curiosity turned into something you can hold. These are the rooms where children and parents make things together, and show the work proudly, finished or not.

Concept render

The sculpture and film set studio, where children go all in.

Clay, scrap timber, paint, costumes, cameras and lights. A room where children build worlds, make a mess and tell their own stories.

We are lucky to have such a creative community. Among our founding families are artists, and friends who work on film sets, who want to help bring this room to life.

Children are born brave. We want to keep them that way.

First attempts

A programme where adults try something new in front of their children: pottery, a first sauna dip, a song, a kayak. Children learn courage by watching us be beginners.

Growing up with you

A club families grow up in, not grow out of.

Toddlers start in soft play and the crèche. Older children move on to the film studio, the kitchen, the lake and the tree houses, with more independence each year. Parents keep a place to work, rest and learn, whatever their children’s ages.

Updates

How it’s coming together.

    Four pillars, and a house shaped around them

    Calm joined curiosity, creativity and courage as our fourth pillar, because parents told us rest matters as much as growth. Every space on this page now sits under one of them.

    Building the app while the estate takes shape

    While the estate finds its final form, we are building the digital side of The Bury: a free app for parents to book spaces and classes, and a place to find books, toys and pre-loved clothes for your children. You will be the first to try it.

  3. Nov 2026

    Design philosophy shared

    We have shared the philosophy, and your ideas, with our architect. The library, family kitchen and co-working space are the first spaces being shaped.

  4. Oct 2026

    Your answers came in

    Founding families told us which spaces and services they would use most. Those answers now sit at the centre of the brief.

  5. Oct 2026

    A fresh start

    After the fire at the main house, work restarted with a new survey and, in many ways, a blank page to design on together.
